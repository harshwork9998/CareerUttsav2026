Good questions, honest answers

What exactly is Career Uttsav?

A career discovery platform for students after 10th & 12th, bringing together students, parents, educators, universities and experts through sessions and real conversations.

Who should attend?

Students in Classes 9–12 deciding on streams or career paths, along with their parents.

What do I actually get as a visitor?

University stalls, expert seminars, a psychometric test worth ₹3,000, a free book worth ₹399, scholarship contests, and direct networking.

Is it useful if I'm still unsure about my future?

Definitely — whether you have a clear goal or are just beginning to explore, it's designed to help you discover the right path.

How can Career Uttsav help parents?

Parents gain insight into modern career options, admission pathways and industry trends.

How can my institution exhibit or partner?

Fill out the Register as an Exhibitor form — our team will guide you through the process.

How do I register?

Tap "Get Your Pass" above, or contact us at info@careeruttsav.in or +91 9900795000.
