Helping students discover the right career path

Career Uttsav is a career discovery platform for students after 10th & 12th. Through expert sessions, workshops, and real conversations, students get the clarity and exposure needed to make confident career decisions, bringing together students, parents, educators, universities and industry experts to bridge the gap between education and opportunity.

  • 🎯 Career guidance from real experts, not textbooks
  • 🚀 Exposure to new-age, emerging career opportunities
  • 🤝 Interactive sessions, workshops & hands-on activities
  • 🎓 Direct insights from universities and professionals
